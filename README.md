# Notes from a Passive-Aggressive Fridge

A one-line entry for the make.fun competition: a single funny, passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner — dry, petty, and specific, the sort of thing a smug appliance would put on its door screen. Running `main.py` prints exactly the entry text and nothing else.

## Entry

Your leftovers have been in here for six days — at this point I am just storing your guilt.
